## For future Claude
Duration measures bond price sensitivity to interest rates. A 10-year bond with duration 8 means a 1% rate rise drops the price ~8%.

## Mechanism
When the Fed raises rates, existing fixed-coupon bonds become less attractive than newly issued bonds at the new higher rate. The price of the old bond falls until its yield matches the new market rate.
